Sets the coding rate.

Parameters:

crate – CR0 | CR1 | CR2 | CR3 | CR4 CRx = 0 to 4 The coding rate RCoding is calculated as follows: RCoding = 4 / (4 + CRx) ‘CR0’ corresponds to no coding, i.e. RCoding = 1.
