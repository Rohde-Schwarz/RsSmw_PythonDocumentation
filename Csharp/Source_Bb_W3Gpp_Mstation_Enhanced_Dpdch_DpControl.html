PowContAssModeEnum Assignment#
// SCPI: [SOURce<HW>]:BB:W3GPp:MSTation:[ENHanced]:[DPDCh]:DPControl:ASSignment
PowContAssModeEnum value = driver.Source.Bb.W3Gpp.Mstation.Enhanced.Dpdch.DpControl.Assignment;
driver.Source.Bb.W3Gpp.Mstation.Enhanced.Dpdch.DpControl.Assignment = PowContAssModeEnum.FDPCh;

Enabled for UL-DTX mode only (method RsSmw.Source.Bb.W3Gpp.Mstation.Udtx.State ON) . The power control recognizes the UL-DPCCH gaps according to 3GPP TS 25.214. Some of the TPC commands sent to the instrument over the external line or by the TPC pattern are ignored, whereas others are summed up and applied later. The processing of the TPC commands depends only on whether the BS sends the TPC bits on the F-DPCH with slot format 0/ slot format 9 or not.

assignment: NORMal | FDPCh
