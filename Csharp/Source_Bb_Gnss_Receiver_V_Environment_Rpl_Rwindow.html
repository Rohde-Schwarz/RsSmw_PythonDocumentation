Sets the repetition window. This window defines the distance of repeated obstacles that means contiguous objects.

repWindow integer Range: 1 to 1E6, Unit: m

vehicle Repeated capability selector

When you do not use any explicit repeated capability selector, the value is taken from its parent group.
