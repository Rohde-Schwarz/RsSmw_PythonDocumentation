Source:Am:Deviation#

SCPI Commands

[SOURce<HW>]:AM:DEViation:MODE

Direct SCPI Members

ModulationDevModeEnum Mode#
// SCPI: [SOURce<HW>]:AM:DEViation:MODE
ModulationDevModeEnum value = driver.Source.Am.Deviation.Mode;
driver.Source.Am.Deviation.Mode = ModulationDevModeEnum.RATio;

Selects the coupling mode. The coupling mode parameter also determines the mode for fixing the total depth.

amDevMode: UNCoupled | TOTal | RATio UNCoupled Does not couple the LF signals. The deviation depth values of both paths are independent. TOTal Couples the deviation depth of both paths. RATio Couples the deviation depth ratio of both paths
