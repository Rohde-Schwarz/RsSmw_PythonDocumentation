Defines how the scheduling and the content of the different PDSCH allocations is defined and performed.

schedulingMode: MANual | AUTO MANual No cross-reference between the settings made for the CORESET DCIs and the PDSCHs settings. Configure the PDSCH allocations manually. AUTO Content and scheduling of the PDSCH according to the configuration of the CORESET DCIs.
