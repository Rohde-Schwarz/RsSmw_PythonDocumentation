The command activates or deactivates channel coding interleaver state 2 for the selected channel. Interleaver state 2 is activated or deactivated for all the transport channels together. Interleaver state 1 can be activated and deactivated for each transport channel individually (command method RsSmw.Source.Bb.W3Gpp.Bstation.Enhanced.Channel.Dpch.Tchannel.Interleaver.Set) . Note: The interleaver states do not cause the symbol rate to change.

interleaver2 ON | OFF

channelNull Repeated capability selector

When you do not use any explicit repeated capability selector, the value is taken from its parent group.
