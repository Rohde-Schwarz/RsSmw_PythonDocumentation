Selects the test signal source.

iqSource: SINE | CONStant | ARB | TTONe SINE Generates a sine waveform. To determine the frequency, use command method RsSmw.Test.Bb.Generator.Frequency.Set(). CONStant Uses a constant I/Q test signal. ARB Selects a signal generated generated by the ARB.
