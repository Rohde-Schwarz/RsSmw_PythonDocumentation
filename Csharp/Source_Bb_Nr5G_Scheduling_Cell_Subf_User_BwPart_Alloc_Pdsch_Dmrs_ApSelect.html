Each layer of a PDSCH allocation is mapped to a certain antenna port. By the command the antenna ports are selected which are used for the transmission of the PDSCH allocation.

pdschApSel: AP1000 | AP1001 | AP1002 | AP1003 | AP1004 | AP1005 | AP1006 | AP1007 | AP1008 | AP1009 | AP1010 | AP1011

cellNull Repeated capability selector

subframeNull Repeated capability selector

userNull Repeated capability selector

bwPartNull Repeated capability selector

allocationNull Repeated capability selector

antennaPortNull Repeated capability selector

When you do not use any explicit repeated capability selector, the value is taken from its parent group.
