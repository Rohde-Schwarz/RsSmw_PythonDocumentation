Sets the type of channelization of 320 MHz channels in the BW field of the U-SIG-1 field. The channelization affects two adjacent 160 MHz of a 320 MHz in the 6 GHz band.

bwType T1_320 | T2_320 T1_320 320MHz-1 channelization with channel center frequency numbers 31, 95 and 159. T2_320 320MHz-2 channelization with channel center frequency numbers 63, 127 and 191.

frameBlock Repeated capability selector

When you do not use any explicit repeated capability selector, the value is taken from its parent group.
