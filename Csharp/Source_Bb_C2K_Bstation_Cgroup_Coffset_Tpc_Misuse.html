The command activates “mis-” use of the power control bits of the selected F-DCCH or F- FCH for controlling the channel powers of these channels. Power control is available for sub channel types F-DCCH and F-FCH. F-DCCH is only generated for radio configurations 3, 4 and 5. The bit pattern (see commands BB:C2K:BSTation<n>:CGRoup<n>:COFFset<n>:TPC…) of the power control bits of each channel is used to control the channel power. A “1” leads to an increase of channel powers, a “0” to a reduction of channel powers. Channel power is limited to the range 0 dB to -80 dB. The step width of the change is defined with the command method RsSmw.Source.Bb.C2K.Bstation.Cgroup.Coffset.Tpc.Pstep.Set. For the traffic channels, this value is specific for the selected radio configuration.

misuse 1 | ON | 0| OFF
