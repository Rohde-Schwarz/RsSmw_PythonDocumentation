Sets an upper limit for the relative noise content in fixed noise filter mode (method RsSmw.Sense.Power.Filter.Type.Set) . This value determines the proportion of intrinsic noise in the measurement results.

nsRatio float Range: 0.001 to 1

channel Repeated capability selector

When you do not use any explicit repeated capability selector, the value is taken from its parent group.
