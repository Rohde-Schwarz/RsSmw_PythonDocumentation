BrsTransPeriodEnum BtrPeriod#

BrsTransPeriodEnum value = driver.Source.Bb.V5G.Downlink.Signals.Brs.BtrPeriod;

Specifies the beam reference signal transmission period signaled via .

transPeriod: P00 | P01 | P10 | P11 P00: single-slot ( 5 ms) , maximum 7 downlink transmitting beams per antenna port P01: single-subframe (= 5 ms) , maximum 14 downlink transmitting beams per antenna port P10: two-subframe (= 10 ms) , maximum 28 downlink transmitting beams per antenna port P11: four-subframe (= 20 ms) , maximum 56 downlink transmitting beams per antenna port
