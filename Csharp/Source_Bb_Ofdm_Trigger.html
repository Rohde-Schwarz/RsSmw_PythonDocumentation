TrigSourEnum Source#
// SCPI: [SOURce<HW>]:BB:OFDM:TRIGger:SOURce
TrigSourEnum value = driver.Source.Bb.Ofdm.Trigger.Source;

Selects the trigger signal source and determines the way the triggering is executed. Provided are the following trigger sources:[[/LISTINTRO]]

  • INTernal: Internal manual triggering of the instrument

  • INTA | INTB: Internal triggering by a signal from the other basebands

  • External trigger signal via one of the local or global connectors:[[LISTINTRO]]

    • EGT1 | EGT2: External global trigger

    • EGC1 | EGC2: External global clock

    • ELTRigger: External local trigger

    • ELCLock: External local clock

  • For secondary instruments (SCONfiguration:MULTiinstrument:MODE SEC) , triggering via the external baseband synchronization signal of the primary instrument: SOURce1:BB:ARB:TRIGger:SOURce BBSY

  • OBASeband | BEXTernal | EXTernal: Setting only Provided only for backward compatibility with other Rohde & Schwarz signal generators. The R&S SMW200A accepts these values and maps them automatically as follows: EXTernal = EGT1, BEXTernal = EGT2, OBASeband = INTA or INTB (depending on the current baseband)

trigSource: INTB | INTernal | OBASeband | EGT1 | EGT2 | EGC1 | EGC2 | ELTRigger | INTA | ELCLock | BEXTernal | EXTernal | BBSY
