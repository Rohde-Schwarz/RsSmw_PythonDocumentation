If enabled, this parameter configures the PDCCH DCI to be transmitted within the common or UE-specific search space.

searchSpace OFF | AUTO | COMMon | UE | ON | 0| 1 COMMon | UE Common and UE-specific search spaces, as defined in the 3GPP specification OFF | AUTO For backwards compatibility only.

subframeNull Repeated capability selector

itemNull Repeated capability selector

When you do not use any explicit repeated capability selector, the value is taken from its parent group.
