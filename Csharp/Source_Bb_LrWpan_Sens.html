silentGap: SG0 | SG1 | SG40

HrpUwbSenspAcketConfigEnum Spc#

Sets the sensing packet configuration.

packetConfig: SPC0 | SPC1 | SPC2 | SPCU SPC0 | SPC1 | SPC2 Provide the sensing packet format according to P802.15.4ab/D02. SPCU Provides no SYNC and no SFD field. The format provides the SENS active packet in between two silent gaps.
