Sets the bandwidth mode of preamble puncturing.

preamblePuncBw BW4 | BW5 | BW6 | BW7 BW4 | BW5 Sets the bandwidth mode for HE80 channels. BW6 | BW7 Sets the bandwidth mode for HE8080 channels.

frameBlock Repeated capability selector

When you do not use any explicit repeated capability selector, the value is taken from its parent group.
