Selects the type of measurement.

Parameters:

polarity – AUTO | SINGle AUTO Continuous measurement. Terminates the measurement in progress if one or both termination criteria are met. Delays the restart of a new measurement until the first measurement result has been queried. The resulting brief measurement interruption is irrelevant because the subsequent measurement is synchronized within 24 data bits. SINGle Single measurement, started with method RsSmw.bert.trigger.immediate.perform() | method RsSmw.bler.trigger.immediate.perform() .
